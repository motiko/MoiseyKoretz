Yurik

L.G-n.

/ "Natasha" /

Musical performance

Little hands touch me by the neck:

Tender touches, light as dreams.

I do not dare to kiss the little hands.

Yurik! My beloved unborn son!

It is not too late for me yet, I still have time.

Only twenty-two, and that only since this spring.

And yet, somehow, touching my neck

Are little hands, tender as dreams.

My Seryozha is intelligent, kind and handsome,

He loves me so, he needs me…

Only it is incomprehensible and unfair:

Why a lover, why not a husband?

Sometimes he is gone for a whole week,-

Anxiety in my heart. Jealousy and anguish.

Why must I, in an empty bed,

Restlessly seek my beloved at night?

Have I really no right to happiness?

Have I a right only to dreams?

Dreams in which, tenderly touching my neck,

Is Yurik, my beloved, unborn son.

1941
