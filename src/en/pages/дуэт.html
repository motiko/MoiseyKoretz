Duet

/ " Natasha" /

Musical performance

Sergei:

Is this it, is this it, is this it the field?

Is that it, is that- it, is that it the snow?

Was it here we walked together with Olya

Last autumn , in November?

The sky was wrapping itself in ragged clouds,

The snow wove patterns on the field,

The wood far off either was, or was not.

I led Olya by the hand across the field.

Snow had woven over the hoops of the field,

Here and there grass could still be seen…

Olya's thin, tender hoops,

As if in jest, I kissed them then.

Fluffs of snow flew in behind the collar

And ran on it like a tear.

Closer, closer –there it is, the city.

" Well, goodbye. I am off home!

The city scattered my fairy tale,

In the ringing of the trams rang it apart.

I shall never be able to find it again

In books, women, or in wine.

Is this it, is this it, is this it the field?

Is that it , is that it , is that –it the snow?

Was it here we walked together with Olya

Last autumn, in November?

Olya:

And yet, he was a fine boy,

A remarkably funny boy he was.

He took each little finger on my hand,

And kissed the very smallest finger.

In the restaurant it is warm and merry,

-The jazz threads song after song.

He sits. He is silent. His head hung down.

…And himself keeps edging closer.

-" So you, Seryozha, will fall asleep soon.

Or does your head ache?"-

- " Olenka, Olya, let us go out of town.

There I shall find the words sooner".

We arrived with him in some field,

In a completely empty place.

- "Well now. Now we two are together, Olya.

If only we could always be together so."

We wandered the field with no road at all,

He kept talking about art,

Until my feet froze,

And it grew dull and sad.

Well, I got bored. I said: -" Goodbye",

And jumped onto the tram as it moved,

And he, the bear, missed the tram!

Well, and that is all there was.

And still, I remember ,looking at the little finger,

At the very smallest finger,

…And yet , he was a fine boy,-

A remarkably funny boy he was!
