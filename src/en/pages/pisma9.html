Letter nine

Komi ASSR, Mezhog - the city of Ivanovo. 31.12.44

Dear Shura!

I congratulate you on the coming New Year (this, at the moment when I am writing this letter) and on our little daughter’s birthday (this, at the moment when you are reading it). I hope that this year will bring us more happiness than the last. The war will end, a more normal life will begin for you, and, perhaps, I will be able to see you both. Once again I thank you for the makhorka you sent. It was very good, and from Irochka’s letter I understood how hard it is for you to get it.

I kiss you warmly, MKorets.
