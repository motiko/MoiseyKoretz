Interrogation record of Kravchenko

Interrogation record

Kravchenko Pavel Andreevich, b. 1903, Ukrainian, member of the CP(b)U, not previously convicted, Kharkov, Chaikovsky 14, apt. 2, 12 December 1935.

I, Kravchenko Pavel Andreevich, born 1903, member of the AUCP(b), Ukrainian, married, never convicted, on the NKVD register as a Chekist.

On the case of Korets I can testify as follows: from the spring of 1935, as soon as it became known that the government was giving very responsible assignments of defense significance and that in connection with this it became necessary to introduce a strict pass system, Korets was the first to begin mocking it, inciting the scientific workers not to recognize any pass system, which led to great scandals. In connection with this Korets put forward the idea that the institute, in connection with the shift to defense topics, was declining theoretically and that its known name abroad would die away, and therefore it was necessary, as the logical conclusion of him, of Korets, and of the other scientific workers — to fight against defense topics. Korets was, in essence, the organizer and the soul of the group that was waging the struggle against defense topics. Korets acted more by quiet sapping, influencing the scientific supervisors, as he himself stated to the commission investigating the Party-Komsomol case. Through them he put demands before the directorate and through them carried them into effect. From this it is clear that Korets personally, for the most part, did not speak out openly. Korets came forward together with Landau with the working out of a wages-rates system in the wall newspaper, which came down to the following: to equalize the wages of all engineers and laboratory assistants regardless of the complexity and the conditions of the work. This leveling had the aim of undermining the work of the special laboratory, for in it people worked with a certain percentage of increase on the wage. In addition, in order to undermine the special work, Korets equated the well-known Professor Slutskin, head of the special laboratory, with a laboratory assistant, declaring: "Slutskin - not a physicist". In order to carry on a complete disorganization of the work of the institute, chiefly of topics of defense significance, Korets and Landau declared to the workers of the institute that they, in particular Korets, demand of the directorate that it lower the wages of the scientific workers until they pass the theoretical minimum. Fighting against defense topics, a group of scientific workers together with Korets often gathered in apartments and discussed some points of a program of struggle against the directorate. Incidentally, there entered here into this group foreigners — Weissberg, Ruhemann Varvara, Ruhemann Martin. Of the scientific supervisors there entered Shubnikov, Landau, Rozenkevich, Obreimov. This group spread rumors everywhere: "the directorate - is a wrecker, making of the institute a public establishment". For three-four months this group uninterruptedly kept the institute in a fever, which told very strongly on the deadlines for carrying out the government assignments, and only thanks to the Bolshevik vigilance of the directorate and of the special workers was it not disrupted.

I can testify to nothing more on this case.
