Afterword

That is all, dear reader.

I have fulfilled a Jew’s duty,

Having told you, reader,

This ancient Haggadah.

Because in our time

A Negro, a Frenchman, a Chinese, a German,

An Ethiopian, a Hindu, a Dane -

Are all a little bit Jews.

I will only add in conclusion:

Time in a furious torrent

Flows across the universe

And carries away irrevocably

Much joy and grief.
