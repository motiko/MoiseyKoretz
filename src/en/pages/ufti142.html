Eleonora Epstein’s statement

To the chairman of the special collegium from Epstein E.L.1

I request that the staff of UFTI be called as witnesses in the case of my husband, Moisey Abramovich Korets: Prof. Landau, Prof. Shubnikov, the director of UFTI, Leipunsky, and Vereshchagin.

21 February 1936

1Eleonora Lazarevna Epstein – wife of M.A. Korets
