Order to close the case

Order. The city of Kharkov, 25 July 1936. I, chief of the 4th Section of the Secret Political Department of the Kharkov Regional Directorate of the NKVD, Senior Lieutenant of State Security Frey, having reviewed the investigation file on the charge against Korets M.A., F O U N D:

that, by the additional investigative measures carried out in the case of Korets, materials in sufficient measure for charging him as an accused have not been obtained, and therefore, guided by Article 97, I have determined:

Case no. 7771 on the charge against Korets Moisey Abramovich is to be discontinued from further action. The measure of restraint selected with respect to the accused, a written undertaking not to leave, is to be revoked, and he is to be informed of this.
