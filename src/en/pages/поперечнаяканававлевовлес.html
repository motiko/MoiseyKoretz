A cross ditch, left, into the woods

L.Yaros.

Musical performance

At picket 1001

The enchanted forest has fallen silent forever,

The blizzard weaves its nets,

And from the violet-gray skies

A gleam fell upon the snows.

A white silence freezes all around.

The pines stand guard over it…

Between the trees a ditch is visible:

Two walk along the ditch.

Covers the frozen parquet of ice

A fluffy carpet of snow.

Behind, the minted print of boots

Spoils the virgin pattern.

The current of fate has stopped:

There are neither minutes nor years…

Somewhere far off was left forgotten

The one thousand and first picket.

“ Someone has stolen the best years.

Is it not true, say?

We have lost the happiness of freedom,

Our life is broken.

The stinking zone – our nature,

Our merriment – grub:

If one lives this way another half a year –

One can go mad!”

“Drop it. My dear! Why then fall

Into such black pessimism.

The head must be held higher,

We ourselves must manage

In this region forgotten by people

To keep our own soul.

The mind must be tempered in this struggle,

And not go out of one’s mind.”

-“ You know , Lidiya, in this crowd

Of camp half-beasts

I am so lonely,

As if there were no people.

There is no one to say a sincere word to,

No one here to love.

No one…no one to kiss,

No one with whom to forget the longing.”

-“ Nonsense. There are many good ones around.

One must manage to find them.

One must look both for friends and for girl friends

On our hard road.

A good soul can be understood,-

Only open your eyes.

And kissing? Kiss me,

And we shall turn back.”

Blue dusk puts out the sunset,

Snow falls slowly.

Pines stand along the edge of the ditch

In the utter silence of sleep.

Taking each other by the hand, they walk back.-

They trample their own track…

Visible far off, through the mica of the fog,

The one thousand and first picket.

NightYurik
