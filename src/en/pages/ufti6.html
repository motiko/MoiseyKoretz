Interrogation record, 2/XII

Interrogation record

2 December 1935

Question: Where did you work before coming to Kharkov?

Answer: Until the year 25, Simferopol, then Moscow, an artel of bookbinders, then an unskilled laborer. In 1927 I entered the Moscow Industrial-Pedagogical Tekhnikum to study. In 1929 I transferred to the Leningrad Physico-Mechanical Institute, where I remained until 1932. From 1932, in Sverdlovsk, a research associate at the Institute of Physics. In 1935, at Landau's invitation, I came to UFTI.
