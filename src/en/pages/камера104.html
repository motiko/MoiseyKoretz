Cell 104

Musical performance

Better to howl like a wolf at liberty.

Better to be a stone in a field.

-----

From the wooden bunks you have hung

Seventy-two heads.

Who are you, who are you? Living or corpses?

Corpses of those pounded in the Lefortovo mortar?

Corpses, like you, -are chary of complaints.

Corpses, like you, - are silent and dull.

Only the corpses have fairer morgues.

The caustic stench of people and the slop bucket

Creeps like mustard gas into your lungs.

Here it is, here it is – the Lefortovo cough.

Who are you? – worms in Limburger cheese?

Who are you – actors in a ghastly satire?

Where do you live, in what world,

Cell one hundred four?

Perhaps you came to me in a dream..?

Then better to howl like a wolf at liberty,

Better to be a stone in a field,

Than to see such dreams.
