Slander

Musical performance

The woman doctor Poklar

Dimitrivna Vera

Has a repulsive genre

And bad manners.

Despite her rank

And her mature years

She captivates men

And is badly dressed.

She occupies her day

Not with treatment, but with nonsense,

And at anyone not too lazy,

She casts glances at them all.

At the doctor's bedside

/I'll touch on it in passing/

Admirers hang about

All four shifts.

And she herself is ugly,

A figure like a heifer's!

Between ourselves, she

Is not a doctor, but a fool,

Why does everyone dislike

This stupid mug?

Probably only for this,

That it does not look like us.

19 May 1946
