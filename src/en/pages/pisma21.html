Letter twenty-one

23.6.49. Mezhog - Leningrad

My dear Irochka! Your previous letter you wrote to me during the examination session before the winter holidays.You promised to write a fuller letter during the holidays...I beg you, write, if only in brief, how the examinations went, what occupies you now, and what your plans are for the summer. From the newspapers I know that at your faculty this year they spent a great deal of time on the question of cosmopolitanism in literature and criticism. Did this discussion touch the students deeply as well? ( They herded all of us into a large auditorium. Young aspirants from the platform blasted their teachers, by the way the most talented and popular ones,for example, Gukovsky. Then those teachers disappeared somewhere.We understood nothing. It was shameful and frightening- E.K.). Are you still in the drama circle? This year I am in a bad mood, so I wrote little.What unsettled my mood most of all was that a possibility had appeared that I would be transferred somewhere to Central Asia or something of that sort, and there I would again have to fight my way through from the very beginning, from general work and so on, and I have less and less strength. P.S. If for certain reasons it is awkward for you to correspond with a person who is in my position, then drop me a line about it, one little word. This will not offend me in the least, since I myself am very worried for you in this regard.
