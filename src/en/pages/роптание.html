Murmuring

Musical performance

From precious modeling clay

God sculpted my body

Only so as to fling it, senselessly,

Under someone's nailed boot.

Having broken his boundless repose

For creation and for love,

Gave me a lofty, pure soul,-

So that one might spit into it. Thoughts, born of me in torment,

Glittering like a diamond,

Rough hands crushed without pity,

Hiding them from those who wait in weary boredom.

My unburned youth is going out,

The indestructible spirit bends…

By this path, - restless, dangerous, -

Lord, I shall not arrive.

Thy will be done, lord, unsearchable are thy ways,

Lord, strengthen my spirit, lord, my despair forgive me…

Just as you, I could have created a universe,

My will shall not yield to yours.

I could, by my inspired thought,

Breathe a soul into millions of people.

What you did in stern dispassion,

I would have made, choking on love.

With a world burning, exultant, new,

Created by a proud, solemn word,

Have replaced this suffering world.

I do not fear your terrible punishment,

I reject your reward,

I ask neither mercy nor a gift, -

I stand before you as before an equal.

Forgive me my pride, lord, send down humility upon your servant,

Lord, upon a wretched servant, black before your bright face, lord,

a handful of dust at your feet.

The cosmos is shackled by space and by cold.

Fragments of worlds have crowded into mountains.

A world that was ruled by pain and by hunger

Spat its last blood out into the sky.

The human race – the vomit of god,-

That was, stinking with the poison of the ages,

Somewhere crushed, far off, at the foot

Of mountains that buried the remains of gods.

To smithereens the burden of time is smashed,

The wheels of fate have stopped,

Torn to shreds against the starry sieve,

The scroll of the future, hidden by god,

In the world the future is to be no more.

Full of an enormous , tormenting happiness,

Master of his boundless pain,

A glacier, into the ice of frozen passion,

I am alone above space.

Where are you, lord, god of my soul. Do not abandon me, lord, I am afraid,

lord, in the desert of my loneliness, lord, do not leave me alone,

do not leave me alone!...

25 April – 6 May 1946
