The case sent back for further investigation

Particular ruling

On the 13th day of May 1936 a special commission of the Kharkov regional court, composed of:

Chairman Kamsky

Members of the collegium Dmitruk and Khmaruk

With secretary Ishchenko

Without the participation of the parties

Having considered in closed session the case of Korets Moisey Abramovich, accused under Art. 97 of the Criminal Code, the special collegium F O U N D:

In the court session, upon the questioning of the witnesses L.D. Landau and L.V. Shubnikov and by the explanation of the defendant Korets himself, it was established:

1). That the director of the institute, Davidovich, for the first time spoke out in the month of June 1935 for dividing the institute into two parts, of which one part of the institute would occupy itself with questions of military topics.

2). That Davidovich himself brought demoralization into the midst of the scientific workers by his inept leadership, and treated the scientific staff rudely.

3). That the defendant Korets allegedly never carried on work toward the disruption of defense assignments, which the former secretary of the Party committee Muzykansky Semyon Petrovich and the scientific worker of the institute Lifshits Evgeny Mikhailovich can confirm.

4). According to the testimony of the witness Kravchenko, that Korets pursued a line toward the disruption of defense assignments, the following persons can confirm it: Gey Vladimir Veniaminovich, assistant to the director for defense work, who stated to Kravchenko that he had sufficient data on the role of Korets in the disruption of defense assignments. These same data can allegedly be confirmed by the chief of the spec. sector Zalivadny Stepan Yakovlevich and the scientific staff Gusak Ivan, Strelnikov Pyotr Ivanovich and Komarov Pyotr Frolovich.

5). After that the spec. collegium of the Supreme Court of the Ukrainian SSR, by its ruling, proposed that the case of Korets be considered a second time without a preliminary further investigation, and that the charge be reclassified — instead of Art. 97 of the Criminal Code, to the articles providing for counterrevolutionary actions, 54-10 part 1, 54-7, 54-14, which contradicts Art. 290 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, since this is a new kind of crime and requires an additional investigation in the bringing of a charge under articles with a higher sanction, and therefore, guided by Arts. 280 and 290 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, the collegium R U L E D:

The hearing of case no. 147 against Korets Moisey Abramovich is to be postponed and the case sent, through the special procuracy, for further investigation, the witnesses indicated having been questioned. As the measure of restraint for Korets, to leave a written undertaking not to leave from the place of the permanent place of residence.
