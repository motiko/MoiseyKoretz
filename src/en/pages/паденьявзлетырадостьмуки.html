Falls, rises, joy, torment

S.R.

Musical performance

Hours of merriment, years of boredom,

The happiness of a meeting, the pain of parting,

The quiet light of love and thought, -

Well, that is all, my dear,

That I could gather over many years,

Puffing and straining my memory,

Your luckless poet.

When, in a moment of dark sadness,

You huddle in a secluded corner,

Perhaps my modest gift

Will remind you of the one

Who, whether near or far,

Forgets nothing…

And once again will not be lonely

The beating of your heart.

18 December 1945
