Letter twenty-two

10.6.50

My dear Irochka!

It will soon be a year and a half that I have had no news from you. Although in that time I wrote five letters...All the same I very much want to know something about you.Who knows what may have happened in that time: in a year and a half I might already have become a grandfather...My situation is the same and will remain the same until March of 52.

I am waiting for an answer. I kiss you.

Your papa. Mezhog.
