A letter

S.G.

Musical performance

My own! After our meeting,

I am drunk with love for you.

You sped away, my proud gyrfalcon,

And again I am quite alone.

But for a moment faces touched

The golden fleece of love:

It was the blue bird of happiness

That gave me twenty minutes.

Without you, even a day in spring

Is like a dark winter night to me,-

Even if I take upon my knees

Our gray-eyed daughter.

Sometimes it is so bitter for me

That I would run off to your post,

Into your front-line gymnasterka,

Just to bury my nose in it for a moment.

You do love me at least a little?

Otherwise I would lose my mind!

You know, our little one today

Said "ma" to me for the first time.

I say to her: "Say also – papa,-

That I am mama, I know that myself."

But she waved her little paw

And again repeated: " mA".

I often see you in my dreams,

I wake and call aloud.

My own! I need you nearer,

I need you now, awake.

Come even for an hour, if you can.

The cup of pain is full past the brim.

Only you can help me stand.

My dear! Come, come!

18 July 1946
