The leaflet

no later than 23 April 1938

Proletarians of all countries, unite!

Comrades!

The great cause of the October Revolution has been basely betrayed. The country is flooded with streams of blood and filth. Millions of innocent people have been thrown into prisons, and no one can know when his turn will come. The economy is falling apart. Famine is drawing near. Do you not see, comrades, that the Stalinist clique has carried out a fascist coup. Socialism remains only on the pages of newspapers that have lied themselves out completely. In his frenzied hatred of real socialism Stalin has made himself the equal of Hitler and Mussolini. Destroying the country for the sake of keeping his power, Stalin is turning it into easy prey for the bestial German fascism. The only way out for the working class and all the working people of our country — is a resolute struggle against Stalinist and Hitlerite fascism, a struggle for socialism.

Comrades, organize! Do not fear the executioners from the NKVD. They are capable only of beating defenseless prisoners, of catching innocent people who suspect nothing, of plundering the people’s property and of inventing absurd trials about conspiracies that do not exist.

Comrades, join the Anti-Fascist Workers’ Party. Make contact with its Moscow Committee.

Organize AWP groups at the enterprises. Set up underground technical work. By agitation and propaganda prepare a mass movement for socialism.

Stalinist fascism holds only by our lack of organization. The proletariat of our country, which threw off the power of the tsar and the capitalists, will be able to throw off the fascist dictator and his clique, Long live 1 May — the day of struggle for socialism!

The Moscow committee of the Anti-Fascist Workers’ Party
