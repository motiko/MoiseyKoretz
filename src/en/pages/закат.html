Sunset

Musical performance

Everything is overgrown with dense taiga,

Needles, needles, needles…

Marshes, moss and humus –

Accursed land.

My accursed fate –

To live out all my days here.

And to know and to feel that I

Am no longer a human being…

A strip of gray sand,

Losing itself in the skies,

Like an endless anguish

Has cut through the forests.

Day and night they haul the sand

Along gangways of planks,

And drink my life, drop by drop,

The taiga, the anguish, the sand…

And in the evening you come to the barrack

Tired and sick,

You wolf down a full pail of balanda,

You chase it with unboiled water,-

Then an animal anguish

Drives you out into the yard again…

And suddenly a blazing sunset

Stops your gaze.

Flooded with bright rays,

A row of spruces stands clear,

And the red trunks of the pines

Burn like torches.

And a dream is born again.

And there are no oppressive thoughts.

Since there is beauty in the world –

There is life, and erga sum!
