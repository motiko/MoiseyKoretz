To the reader

Respected reader!

I ask your forgiveness,

That today, for conversation,

I chose a rhythm long since known.

Gitche Manitou the mighty,

Who created all the peoples,

In this rhythm to Hiawatha

Gave much counsel of great worth.

With this rhythm the stormy petrel,

Like unto black lightning,

Now with a wing touching a wave,

Now rising like an arrow to the clouds,

Flew on in a four-foot

Marvelous trochee,

Though the words were not broken

As is the custom, into lines.

In this rhythm Heinrich Heine

Sang Jewish motifs,

Parodic ballads

And Spanish canzones.

Having taken this wondrous rhythm,

Having taken a plot from the ancient Torah,

Having taken irony from Heine, -

I cooked up this dish.

But when at the bottom of the pot

You find pain and bitterness, -

Know, reader, this bitterness

I did not go borrowing anywhere.
