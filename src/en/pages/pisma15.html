Letter fifteen

23.3.47, Mezhog– Leningrad

My dear little daughter!

Today we have the first quiet, sunny day. In the morning it was 25 degrees of frost, and now it is dripping from every roof. Today is my day off. In the morning I tried to work, and then weakness, a consequence of my illness, forced me to lie down. And I came to want very much to see you, to hold your hand, to talk with you. The older you grow, the closer you are to me, and the dearer, and the harder it is without you. And it has already been three months with no letters from you.

Write, Irusha. Your letters are a great joy to me.

Greetings to Mama. I kiss you warmly, your papa Misha.
