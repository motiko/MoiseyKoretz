A reference from UFTI

A reference

Korets Moisey Abramovich, born in 1908 in the family of an artisan in the city of Sevastopol, an employee, a Jew, non-Party, higher education.

At UFTI Korets arrived from the Ural Physico-Technical Institute on 13.03.35 and took up work as an engineer of the theoretical group; in this post he worked until 14.11.35, i.e. until his dismissal from the institute for concealment of his soc. origin. As a specialist physicist Korets was of no value to UFTI. Earlier he was twice expelled from the Komsomol for hiding his social origin and for anti-Komsomol behavior. At UFTI before the arrest he was also expelled from the Komsomol for concealment of his soc. origin, for a break with the Komsomol organization and for an attempt to discredit the defense workers. He was an active participant in a group whose struggle against the directorate and the defense workers struck at the fulfillment of UFTI’s defense program. Korets, covering himself with his Komsomol card, in the most active way knocked this group together, creating the impression that this group’s actions were coordinated with the Komsomol and Party organizations. He advanced the proposition that the scientific level of UFTI is declining, because much work of a technical character is being carried on (at that time orders had been received to increase work of a defense kind), demanded lower pay for the staff who carried out defense assignments, defending leveling of wages, ridiculed the workers who carried out defense assignments, etc. In general Korets’s work was directed at disruption of the thematic and financial plan and, in particular, of assignments of defense importance.
