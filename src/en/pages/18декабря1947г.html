18 December 1947

S.R.

Musical performance

The sun is going down,

Burning like a yellow candle,

In scarlet flame across the sky

The glow broke into play,-

In the bright colors of sunset

The enchanted pine wood,

In tender rosy patches

The snow’s white carpet.

In the soft , spectral light

Of the burnt-out day

You are beautiful today,

My dear!

You sit beside me,

As if already from long ago.

And like the singing of a flute

Our mute conversation.

And no one will be able

Either to know, or to understand,

How much in this silence

Of golden fire.

I catch with my gaze

Your pensive look,

And in the soul there sounds

A solemn chorus of feelings.

Let the coming year go on,

Forging our closeness…

Give me your hand for happiness,

My dear!
