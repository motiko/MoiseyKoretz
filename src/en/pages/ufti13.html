The indictment

An indictment on the charge against Korets Moisey Abramovich of committing the crimes provided for by Article 97 To the secret-political department of the administration for state security for Kharkov Region there came information that Korets Moisey Abramovich, while working at the Kharkov Physico-Technical Institute, is abusing his official position and is conducting agitation among the workers of the institute against the carrying out of assignments of defense significance. On the basis of the foregoing, Korets was arrested, and a preliminary investigation was begun with regard to him. Questioned in the capacity of the accused, Korets did not admit himself guilty. However, by the investigative actions that were carried out the guilt of Korets has been fully confirmed.

Questioned in the capacity of a witness, Kravchenko testifies that Korets carried on disorganizing work in the institute, agitating for the disruption of defense work. Saying that the institute, in connection with the shift to defense topics, was declining theoretically, and that therefore it was necessary to fight against this. Similar testimony with regard to Korets was given by the witness Davidovich, confirming that the accused conducted agitation against the institute’s carrying out of assignments of defense significance. Demanded on this ground a lowering of the wages of the workers engaged in carrying out work of defense significance. The witness Pyatigorsky testified that Korets, conducting agitation against the carrying out of defense work, grouped around himself persons from among the foreign specialists, setting such persons against the leadership of the institute. The witness Pyatigorsky also confirmed his testimony at the confrontation with the accused Korets. Thus the guilt of Korets in the work he conducted toward the disruption of the carrying out of assignments of defense significance has been fully confirmed.

On the basis of the foregoing there is charged

Korets Moisey Abramovich, b. 1908, a native of Sevastopol, with a higher education, Jewish by nationality, a citizen of the USSR, not previously convicted, a former Komsomol member, the son of an artisan, before his arrest a scientific worker of the Kharkov Physico-Technical Institute, in that:

a) He conducted disorganizing work among the staff of the institute toward the disruption of the carrying out of assignments of defense significance, that is, in the commission of the crimes provided for by Article 97.

On the charge brought against him he did not admit himself guilty.

The charge is confirmed by the testimony of the witnesses Kravchenko, Davidovich, Pyatigorsky.

RESOLVED:

Case no. 7771 on the charge against Korets Moisey Abramovich is to be sent to the prosecutor for special cases of the Kharkov regional procuracy.

Note

The accused Korets is held in custody in the Kharkov prison. There is no material evidence in the case. All the documents, according to the inventory, are attached to the case.
