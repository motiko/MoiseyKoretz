I have forgotten times and dates

L.G-n

Musical performance

I have forgotten times and dates

And the names of cities,

Where they once kissed me:-

I do not want to remember.

All the same,- they have flown past

Nine tenths of a fate…

Why then you, beloved,

Can I not so forget?

Why for you will I bear everything

And, along a mad curve,

Bear myself out into many-faced life

By the one last tenth,

So that, flaring in a last glow,

I may dare to scatter thought,

So that with your brown eyes

Death would see me.

1943
