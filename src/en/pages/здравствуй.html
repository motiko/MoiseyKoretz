Hello

Musical performance

Again,

Again the train,

Again the distant lands.

Hello, morning,

Hello, sun,

Hello, my youth!

Along the carriages the wind whistles,

Tears the black smoke to shreds,-

It is good in this world

To be, like the wind, young.

It is good, like the wind, to race

Into the unknown distance

With the ringing morning

Neck and neck

Running past the towns.

Past the garden,

Across the field,

Only behind, the dust swirls.

Hello, joy,

Hello, freedom,

Hello, tomorrow,

Hello, life.

13 April 1947

FerganaThe heady scent of spring grasses
