Order to bring charges

Order in case 7771

the city of Kharkov, 27.11.1935

I, Lisitsky, operations officer and chief of the 2nd Section of the NKVD of the Ukrainian SSR, having reviewed the investigation material on the charge against Moisey Abramovich Korets, born in 1908, an engineer at UFTI in the city of Kharkov, of the crimes under Arts. 54-11, 54-14, 54-10 of the Criminal Code of the Ukrainian SSR, have found that the actions carried out have established that M.A. Korets is a participant in a counterrevolutionary underground group, conducts demoralizing work among the staff of UFTI, and engages in counterrevolutionary agitation.

RESOLVED:

To charge M.A. Korets as an accused.

Lisitsky

Agreed. Gozdin

I approve. Karlson

The order has been announced to me

11 December 1935 Korets
