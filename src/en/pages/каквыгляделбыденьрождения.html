What V.D.P.’s birthday would have looked like if she had been well

Musical performance

I come in to you quietly,

I’ll be alone, I think:

"Permit me to congratulate you

On the day of your name day."

Suddenly they bring a telegram:

The lord of cherished thoughts

Has taken it into his head to congratulate Verochka

Wholesale, for five name days.

Barely alive, but full of passion,

In comes a fiery Georgian:

" Permit me to congratulate You

On the day of Your ymynin".

Banging the door against the pier,

A drunk barged in, son of a bitch!

" P-p-permit me to congratulate You

On the day of Y-y-your name day."

And after him, with a bouquet of hairpins,

A tonnish, languid gentleman:

" Pepmette moi congratulate

Par jour of Your name day".

With a thick tome of dark prose

A new Paladin enters:

" Permit me to congratulate You

On the day of Your name day".

From a hundred versts a toothy one turned up ,

A modestly insolent harlequin,

And through his teeth congratulates

On the day of Your name day.

And the assignment clerk , later than the rest,

In his dashing hand

Dashes off, in Lermontov’s manner,

Name-day verses.

How many of them, what drives them on!

Spleen comes over me…

Permit me to clear off

To the devil from Your name day.

20–21 May 1946
