All paths are closed to us

Musical performance

All paths are closed to us

And the trails are not beaten,

And our spine crunches

Under the wheel of history.

And on our face is the seal

Of despair before death,

And it is time for us to finish with life,

Like ice at the time of thawing.

But sometimes from the dark clouds

Onto our unsteady lives

A cheerful ray of the sun will gleam

With springtime smiles.

The young scent of lilac

Will seep to us on the wind,

Or I shall meet with you,

And again one will want to live.

20 May 1946
