Prayer

Dedicated to Grevtsev

/ "Natasha"/

Musical performance

Lord, Lord, Lord God!

I will pray to you unceasingly:

-Let your spirit help the ailing heart

Fervently to believe, forgive, and love.

No, I do not ask for wealth and glory,

Strength, intellect, the respect of people,-

Only deliver me from the poison

Of lies, hypocrisy, and spiteful passions.

God of mercy, God of absolution,

This plea I make without wearying:

- Let me feel the joy of forgiveness,

Let me feel your will.

1939
