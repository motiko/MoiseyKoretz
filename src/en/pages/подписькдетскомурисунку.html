Inscription on a child’s drawing

Musical performance

/ a copy in verse /

The wind blows on the ship,

Raising ripples on the water,

The sun shines with its mouth wide open,

Well, and the smoke the other way.

The blue sea is roaring,

The steamer smokes at full blast,

In the boat the oars have broken,

The sailors took fright,-

Chizh climbed onto the deck

And signals: sos/

And to help them, off to one side,

A swan swims with a cygnet.

12 May 1946
