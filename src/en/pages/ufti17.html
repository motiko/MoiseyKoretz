Minutes of the bureau

Kharkov regional committee of the CP(b)U.

Minutes no. 91, item 15 of the closed session of the bureau

of 14 October 1935.

Heard: The Ukrainian Physico-Technical Institute. Rep. comrade Musulbas.

Resolved:

1. To note the considerable contamination of the Ukrainian Physico-Technical Institute with class-hostile and counter-revolutionary elements.

2. In connection with this, to consider it necessary to carry out a purge of the institute, in connection with which to raise before the CC of the CP(b)U and the People’s Commissariat of Heavy Industry the question of assigning a special representative to take part in carrying out the purge of the institute of class hostile elements.

Secretary of the regional committee Musulbas
