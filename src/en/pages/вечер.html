Evening

/ " Natasha"/

Musical performance

Seryozhenka, may I? But you’re already in bed!

How naughty, - you couldn’t wait!

Well, all right, if you’re down, then lie there now,

And you know, finish me that novella,

The one you began yesterday. Well, I’m ready to listen

Quietly, like a little mouse, - I won’t say a word…

…Well, all right, kiss me, but only just once…

Well, you may on the nose. Well, you may on the eye…

You silly! Of course, of course I love you!

Well, if you like, I’ll say it to you a hundred times!

…Seryozha, why won’t you let me sit down!...

Seryozha, Seryozhenka. – you’ll tear the dress!

Don’t be angry with me, Sergei,

I do love you, I love you, and of course I am yours!

…Seryozha, Sergunka, you’re out of your mind!

Don’t! Well, tomorrow I’ll come myself.

Seryozha , Seryozhenka!...What is force for!

Don’t…it hurts…oh, it hurts…O, God!

Seryozha, Seryozha, Seryozha, Seryozha,

Seryozha. Seryozha, Seryozha, Seryozha…

Enough, Seryozha…Enough. That’s enough…

What’s wrong, my dear? Does it hurt you too?...

Seryozha, my sweet, my beloved, my own!

Kiss me harder! Kiss me, darling!

Yes. No. It’s nothing…I’m crying just a little…

Yes, no! It doesn’t mean anything at all,-

For you are my beloved, for you are my dear.

But only…But only…why did you use force!
