From Heine

Musical performance

Often I thought in despair,

That anguish would break me,-

Still I bore all the sufferings,

But do not ask how.

I gave myself a promise –

Anguish shall not break me!

And I bore all the sufferings,

But do not ask how!

4–5 July 1946
