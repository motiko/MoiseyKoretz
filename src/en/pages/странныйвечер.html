A strange evening

V.D.P.

Musical performance

What a strange evening this is,

What a strange longing.

I tried to seek a caress

In a soul that seemed human.

But you have a longing of your own,

And mine is no concern of yours,-

You did not look at me,

You did not wish to caress me.

And you did not show your pain,

And you did not take in mine.

With firm steps you walked away

And did not even press my hand.

And I needed so little

For the longing not to be foolish:

The clasp of a tender hand

And the caress of a friendly glance.

With me there remained the long evening,

And a dusky longing…

I need not have sought

Either these eyes or this meeting.

4 May 1946
