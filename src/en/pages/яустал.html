I am tired

Musical performance

I am tired. The road is endless.

Sweat and dust have sealed my lips.

I am tired. I want to rest.

I am tired.

The stream of ringing songs has run dry,

The singer’s throat has dried up,

And no one will hand me water,-

If only to wash the blood from the corners of my mouth!

I lay down in the dust of the road,

And my soul is empty for the first time.

No strength to love, nor to murmur.

I am tired.

Do not be angry with me, my God,

That I have left your post,-

I have walked so many roads

And today I am tired unto death.

I will rest on the breast of the earth,

I will tell her my trouble,

I will lie half an hour in the dust

And rise again and go on.

6 July 1946

The priest“A Doll’s House”
