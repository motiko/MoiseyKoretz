Dusky embraces

Musical performance

Clouds cast their shadow on the earth.

The wind reddens the sky with the sun.

Onto the ears of grain the winds poured

Golden tales that were true,

Of Dazhbog, or of Kolyada,

Blew from beyond the dark distance.

Secretly, like a beggar,

Someone's lips seek hands…

Onto someone else's shoulders

A swift glance fell, like a falcon.

A swift glance fell, like a falcon,

Onto someone else's shoulders,

And lying in wait for it

Was the caress of dark, warm eyes…

The eyes called it after them

Into the golden valleys of the distance…

Only in a dream can I understand

Dusky embraces,

Only in a dream can I be like that..

Lips found lips…

The wind lays its path farther on.

I toss and turn in bed,

The blanket has slipped to the floor,

Forty-eight mouths were breathing,-

There crept toward my heart

The blind murk of other people's words.

The blind murk of other people's words

Would not let me fall asleep again,

Would not let me see again

The joy of the young sun.

Only in a dream can I understand

Dusky embraces.
