Letter two

...The years of evacuation. Constant undernourishment .Mother and I go about the villages. we trade thread ,needles for food, and at last we get stuck in the village of Gorbovo for a long time.

22.03. 44., Komi A.S.S.R., Mezhog - Ivanovo

...The humiliating and corrupting feeling of hunger. You have probably read K. Hamsun " Hunger". If not, then be sure to read it. In general I recommend that you read every work of Hamsun's you can manage to get. My comrades and I, at your age, were extremely taken with Norwegian literature, especially Ibsen and Hamsun. I love everything of theirs, but what I liked most in Knut Hamsun were two long stories, Under the Autumn Stars and A Wanderer Playing on Muted Strings. In mood these stories are like moonlight on a winter night. When you find these books, be sure to write me about each one you have read. If a person loves and knows how to read Ibsen, then willy-nilly he must grow into an inwardly honest person. In that respect Ibsen is stronger, and reaches further, than even Lev Tolstoy.

...Of your work and of all sorts of skills I will say nothing bad ( in the village I learned to weed out the weeds ,to milk a cow,to harness a horse and so on.- E.K.), but having them can be of use only by chance. A small remark about people. It is not true that there are more bad people than good. In most people, if one looks at them attentively, there is much that is good, and it depends on you yourself whether people turn toward you their good side or their bad. And the main thing is to look at every action of your own from the ethical side, and an inner feeling ( and Russian literature) will unerringly tell you how you ought to act ... I am still working as a surveyor. The chief trouble is with smoking, so if it is possible, send a small parcel with smokes of any quality. I think that in the village one can get "samosad".

A big hello to Mama .I will never forget her friendly feeling toward me in these years that were hard for me.
