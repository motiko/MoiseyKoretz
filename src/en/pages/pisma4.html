Letter four

....( date unknown)Komi ASSR

... So far we have had no frosts, and the temperature stays at 5-10 degrees below zero. The full moon has only just passed, and when in the evening you walk to your lair, you wish it were not 200 meters from the office, but two kilometers away . Since childhood the moon at night , and especially in winter, has made a great impression on me. When its blue ghostly light spills over the sparkling white snow, sharply minting the shadows of sparsely scattered little houses and posts, a sweet and aching longing begins to ring in the soul, on a high violin note, for something that never was and never will be, and one wants to stop one's own being and dissolve into the beautiful stillness of nature. I do not know about others, but I clearly understand why dogs howl at the moon, and I always want to lie down on the snow and join their united chorus, and only the sense of irony, which remains even in these minutes, makes me keep the dignity of a biped and stops me from doing it.

And now a longing for freedom and for you mingles with the objectless lunar longing. And yet I love this feeling .There is something cleansing in it. But it is hard to be alone on a moonlit night...
