Letter ten

...Mezhog - the city of Ivanovo 26.4.45.

Good day, Shura!

I am at fault, of course, before you, but evidently my letter, sent in February, got lost somewhere, or else the gap would not have been so long. All the same, that is no excuse. Nor is it an excuse that these last months I have been working a great deal.Now, besides secretarial work for the chief physician( I have now left the Scientific - research laboratory for the study of vitamin-deficiency diseases) and a little scientific-statistical work, I also have to take some part in the treatment work. Besides that I am preparing to sit the tests for male nurse, and I have also got carried away with work in the drama circle ( this in my old age!) I spin at full stretch all day, from 5 in the morning until 11 at night...

My health is neither good,nor bad.I eat comparatively decently, and Father too, taking it from himself, supplies me with a thing or two. He has returned to Kharkov and is working again in a watchmaker's shop, but his health has become past all bearing, and his eyes too get in the way of the work. Mama is alive, but she has aged greatly. She lives with Tanya's children in Kazakhstan. Tanya is at the front. A major.She was wounded. Lipa and Arkady are working in Saratov, but things are very bad with them. But in any case it is a great happiness that all are alive. Of Galochka I know nothing, whether she is alive, and where. You write that your material situation has now improved, but you do not write how and why.I beg you earnestly, write about this in more detail, write where and as what you are working , whether your mama is alive, where your brothers are. That inclusion on the list for early release, about which I wrote you last year, has brought no results. What to hope for now, I do not know. Unless the end of the war brings something... If any come to hand, then send me some good plays.Better classical ones. And parcels, not in any, do not send, better do something for yourself and for Irochka.
