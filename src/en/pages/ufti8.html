Interrogation record, 22/XII

Interrogation record

22 December 1935

Question: You are charged in that, being an employee of UFTI, you abused your official position, carried on work against the institute’s fulfillment of assignments of defense importance, declaring that special defense work lowers its theoretical level, demanding a cut in the pay of workers engaged in special defense work, grouping around yourself on this basis other persons, and also foreign specialists, that is, in your having committed the crimes provided for by art. 97 of the Criminal Code of the UkrSSR; do you admit yourself guilty of this?

Answer: I do not admit myself guilty of the charge brought against me, that is, of my having committed the crimes provided for by art. 97 of the Criminal Code of the UkrSSR; as for my talks against the fulfillment of the defense work, I did hold such talks, only not against its fulfillment, but against that organization of the direction of the fulfillment of the defense wo rk which the leadership of the institute had set up, in particular the director of the institute, Davidovich, and, in my opinion, such an organization lowered both the theoretical level of the institute and the quality of the fulfillment of the defense work itself.

Question: What else can you add to your testimony?

Answer: There is nothing more to add.

Kozhevnikov
