What will be

S.R.

Musical performance

When dawn, having painted out the stars,

Embosses a fir tree in the window,

When the cool fresh air

Surrounds the warm bed,

I, having left my dreams serenely,

With eyes full of love,

Shall see your tender nape

And your wondrous shoulders.

But I shall not disturb you

And only softly, as in a dream,

Kiss the silken skin

At the dear little hollow on your back.

Having got up carefully from the bed,

Having closed the wide window,

I, as quietly as ever I can,

Wake my little son.

With him the most serious compact

Was made already in times long past,

That to wake Mama in the morning

He alone has the right.

Crawling, as though Papuans,

At a noise drawing back,

He and I lay a route

Under the table, behind the armchair, under the bed.

But at last we are behind the little bed,

And now, lifted by his father,

With his tiny little paw

He will touch you upon the face.

But you woke long ago,

Yet, so as not to spoil the game,

You stretched sweetly in the bed,

As though in a light half-sleep.

But there is no holding back:

" Am!" - caught the little hand in her mouth,

And now both son and mother laugh,

Tousling the bed head over heels.

And the sun looks: what people are these

Laughing, the three of them, by the window?...

Well then, my dear, " what will be",

When the times are fulfilled. -
