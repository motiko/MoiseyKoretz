S.Rus

Musical performance

The eighth floor, an English lock

And on the lock the inscription VAlE

And thoughts, like the steps, are slippery

And the floors, like eight years.

Like eight years of my sorrow,

Which you gave me,

When I was stung to death

By the fire of cold blue eyes.

How well you knew how to be cruel

In your fifteen proud years,

When to the blue-eyed princess

I, the fool, dedicated a sonnet.

Years burst into life rejoicing,

And I grew eight years older,

But I still yearn as before

For the gold of your hair.

And so as to hear the tender voice,

So as to be with you even an hour,

Again I climb hopelessly

To the eighth floor above the clouds.

17 May 1946
