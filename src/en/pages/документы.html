Veritasium. “I finally saw quantum mechanics with my own eyes…”, 4 November 2026. A video about superfluid helium. From 43:46: Landau is called a genius theorist. The video says there was some truth to the charge: citing KGB files, it says he was accused of heading a counter-revolutionary organization. From 44:13: the arrest on 28 April 1938, the Lubyanka, and Kapitsa’s letter to Stalin. Korets is not named.

Films and broadcasts

“Landau — Three Conditions of Happiness. The Accident That Split a Genius’s Life in Two”, channel “Historical Profile”, 12 October 2026. From 29:56: his Kharkov acquaintance, the physicist Moisey Korets, writes a leaflet for 1 May. From 30:22: Korets himself, many years later, confirmed his authorship. From 31:06: Korets and the physicist Yuri Rumer are taken with him. Korets will spend almost 20 years in the camps and in exile. From 1:10:25: the author was most likely Korets, and Landau may have taken part in the editing. This is a video essay, not a television film.

Valeriya Korennaya. “Lev Landau: ‘All That Is Boring Is Very Harmful’. Part 2”, the program “Wings from the Attic”, Davidson Radio, broadcast of 7 November 2023. From 11:36: the leaflet became the cause of the simultaneous arrest of three physicists, Rumer, Korets and Landau.

“Landau, Theorist and Man”, channel “NB KhGU”, 18 April 2018. From 17:32: the speaker reads the story of the leaflet. The sentence is the same story: April 1938, the leaflet, the arrest of Landau, Korets and Rumer.

“The Smartest Jew in the USSR: A Genius Who Was More Dangerous Than a Weapon”, December 2025. In the automatic captions, while the video was open: “Korets claims that you took part in composing this text” and “Korets has already given testimony.” The video is now closed, and the minute was not fixed.

Boris Gorobets. “The Film ‘My Husband Is a Genius’ and Waves in the Cultural Layer”, “Notes on Jewish History”, no. 12 (103), December 2008. The screenwriter, whom Gorobets calls Maria Radzinskaya, said: “And about Kharkov, about Korets, I had to take that out.” This is the Channel One film “My Husband Is a Genius”, 2008.

The automatic captions of “Lev Landau. More Than Love” (the Kultura channel) do not contain the surname. “Ten Commandments of Landau” and “Dau the Magnificent” have no subtitles, so the spoken words were not checked. Igor Pykhalov, “Why Was L. D. Landau Arrested?” has no subtitles. The surname there is in a viewer’s comment.
