White night

Musical performance

The heart flutters like a bird, in alarm,

Sleep flies away from the eyes,-

Neither to lie nor to dream is possible

Through the endless white night.

The stars scattered thinly in the sky,

They took fright at the double dawn.

Listen to the call of forgotten ancestors,

Who live in your scarlet blood.

They have mutinied and climb up together,

There is no holding them now,

And they have no need of you at all

On this strange white night

As hairy shadows they leap.

They bare a mouth smeared with blood,

And you are drawn, unrestrainably

Into their seething round dance.

They thirst to live your life,

This herd of mighty males,-

And with fear and with pride in each

You recognize your own face.

It is you, defending the cave,

Where your cubs were squealing,

Strangled a panther with your hands

And knocked the fangs from a wolf.

It is you, merciless, slant-eyed,

Not having waited for the fight to end,

Dragged a female off by the braids,

Fighting them off with one hand.

It is you, alone, for hours

On a white night sat by the fire

Ground your teeth, growing savage,

Having failed to understand something.

And even then you were troubled

By the unfading light of this night…

And in our anguish we cannot understand,

What a hundred thousand years did not understand.

But no one believes our torment,

And no one will be able to help…

And solitary beasts are snarling

Into the merciless white night.

19 June 1946
