[in the margin] 2/SU 13 EXTRACT from the interrogation record of ROZENKEVICH Lev Viktorovich Of 12-13 August 1937. "In 1933, to the city of Kharkov, to work at the Ukrainian Physico-Technical Institute, there came from the city of Sverdlovsk an engineer- physicist KORETS, who also joined our c.-r.organization. The Trotskyist influence of WEISSBERG on the members of our c.-r.group was defined most clearly in the days of the murder of KIROV and at the announcement in the newspapers of the results of the subse- quent trials. In conversations with us, WEISSBERG sharply condemned the reply of the par- ty, of the Soviet government and the anger of the people at the murder of KIROV, expressing outrage at the cruelty of the reprisal, both against the partic- ipants in the murder and in general against the repressions of the Soviet government. WEISSBERG and after him LANDAU and KORETS openly called on us to be ready to take revenge on the Soviet govern- ment for the repressions. Members of the group often gathered at the apartments of WEIS- SBERG, of SHUBNIKOV and in LANDAU's office at the institute, and discus- sed questions of the trials of the participants in the uncovered Trots- kyist-Zinoviev center and the parallel anti-Sov- iet center. Present at the meetings were SHUBNIKOV, WEISSBERG, LAN-

At these meetings, along with political questions, we discussed methods of carrying out wrecking measures which most ef- fectively could have led to the collapse of the activity of the institute. We also discussed the question of the necessity of hounding and of the scientific isolation of persons who worked on defense assignments and did not share our line... Along with this SHUBNIKOV, with the help of members of the orga- nization - LANDAU and KORETS, by provocation and hounding, dro- ve out of the cryogenic laboratory the senior scientific wor- ker RYABININ, who was engaged in solving practical problems concerning the obtaining of new kinds of fuel for en- gines of internal combustion. Driven to despair, RYA- BININ beat LANDAU, and in connection with this was forced to leave the institute altogether". ROZENKEVICH.
