Interrogation record, 9/XII

Interrogation record

9 December 1935

Question: The investigation has established that you, while working at UFTI, carried out work aimed at disrupting assignments of defense importance; set these facts out in detail for the investigation.

Answer: I carried out no work to disrupt defense assignments at UFTI. As for the reorganization of our institute, that is, its division into three institutes, I did hold conversations with my scientific workers, considering that this was a positive side of my proposal; this conversation I held with the scientific worker Landau and others.

Kozhevnikov
