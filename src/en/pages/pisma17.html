Letter seventeen

Mezhog . 15.6.47

Dear little daughter!

Yesterday I received your letter of 29.5 and since one question of that letter of your letter requires an urgent answer, I am answering you briefly. The VUZ, now, you will pass the examinations, I believe you that examinations at a VUZ are easier than at school, but one must not forget that at school, whether you pass them well or poorly, but once you do pass them, school is finished. At a VUZ, though, for one place there are several several candidates: participants in the war and medalists, among whose number, to you,unfortunately, you do not belong, go without examinations and outside the competition, so that very few places will be left for you.Therefore one must think not only of how to spend the free month better, but of passing the competitive examination better. As far as I have understood, you are planning to go into the Russian section of the philology faculty. You are hardly likely to have a chance of getting in if on the written paper in literature you again have a four...You will have time to travel both south and east during the VUZ holidays. And now you must prepare hard for the examinations, even if as a result of this you grow still thinner. Now about the trip to grandfather and to grandmother, to a dustier and more boring, cham Leningrad with its Neva and its seashore, Kharkov. They would always have been glad of you, but now, I am afraid, your coming will be hard for them. The thing is that, as an artisan, my papa receives almost nothing on the ration cards, and prices at the market are high. In July the new harvest will yet tell. If you were meaning to go in September after the harvest, which, as I hope, will be better than last year's, then there is no need to ask anyone, since I am sure that papa and Anna Vladimirovna will always be glad of you. They love you very much... Well, and now I shall dream a little. In August the potatoes will begin to ripen here too, so if a monotonous potato menu does not frighten you, you could feed yourself here for two weeks. You could stay with acquaintances of mine. For one young girl a place can always be found. You would need money only for the journey here and back. From Leningrad to us ( Madmas station of the North-Pechora main line) there is a through carriage.

The country here is not bad, on the bank of the river Vychegda, but it is a terrible backwater. The village where you spent two years is a capital compared with the settlement of Protoka, of 10 or 12 shacks. So it would be very dull for you. We could see each other once, for a few hours. True, if you had another surname, not mine, we could meet several times and stay longer, but since your surname and mine are the same, we shall have to meet only officially, that is, once. And perhaps it will be possible to prevail upon the authorities for more as well. I should very much like to tell you about myself, to look at you. And it would do you no harm to look closely at our life. But all this, of course, is only a dream, and can hardly come true... (This will come true only in five years) Did Mama receive my letter? I kiss you warmly my dear little daughter.

Your papa Misha.
