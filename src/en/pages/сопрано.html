Trio

Ernst P.

V.D.P.

Zoya G.

Musical performance

Soprano:

How did this come into your head,

How could it only have seemed to you,

That you had clung to something new,

That you could not give me up for good?

Was it not to me, who loved so achingly,

Your life given forever by fate?

Do you remember,- a blinding radiance lit

The many-colored glow of our love.

Thousands of kilometers rose like a wall,

Years of suffering divided us,-

But hewn from imperishable granite

Is the plinth of the stately building of love.

Ah, how tormentingly I searched for you,

How many roads have been walked after you!

Here, I have come, and I call commandingly.

Here, I take what was allotted me by God.

Tenor:

For one smile of yours,

For one promise of love,

Like an infant's cradle

I turned my life upside down.

For one smile of yours,

For one promise of love,

For a flimsy hope I cast aside

A stretch of solid life.

For one smile of yours,

And for laughter tenderer than a violin,

And for a black braid,-

The one that rose between us,

I am not afraid, and I am not sorry.

Growing cold, the hands themselves

Will plunge a dagger into her throat.

For one smile of yours,

For one promise of love,-

The golden fish of happiness

I carry in a trembling heart.

Alto:

What is this, what then? For what is this to me?

My own happiness I gathered crumb by crumb,

I did not steal another's happiness,

I was cruel to no one.

What I held firmly in my hands

Suddenly seeped as dust between my fingers,-

The incredible became a fact.

You, who came out of the dead past,

Trampling my soul along the way,

Do you know that I survived here,

How I got him back on his feet?

God, for what are such torments mine!

His heart is colder than stone.

Lord! You see,- children's hands

Beg you to spare their mama!

No. She led him away. And will not look back.

Let it be. Go. Only know that till the grave

My pain will trail along after you.

15–23 June 1946
