Popular science articles

M. Korets and L. D. Landau. “The Bourgeoisie and Modern Physics”, “Izvestia”, 23 November 1935. Wikipedia says that they wrote the text together and took it to Bukharin. In the reprint there is one signature: Landau.

M. Korets. “The electron microscope”. “Tekhnika Molodezhi”, 1937, no. 9, pp. 35–37.

E. Zelikovich and M. Korets. “With three twos”, a puzzle story. “Tekhnika Molodezhi”, 1937, no. 9, pp. 61–62.

M. A. Karev. “Do Quarks Exist?”. “Priroda”, 1966, no. 2, pp. 85–95.

S. V. Vladimirov, M. A. Karev. “Beyond the Two-Hundred-Degree Barrier. Directed Synthesis of Heat-Resistant Polymers”. “Priroda”, 1966, no. 6, pp. 25–31.

S. V. Vladimirov, M. A. Karev. “Information and Us”. Moscow, “Znanie”, 1970, 192 pp. Series “The People’s University”.

M. Korets, Z. Ponizovsky. “Why Is the Moon Not Made of Cast Iron?”, “Kvant”, 1972, no. 4.
