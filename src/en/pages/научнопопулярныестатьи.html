Popular science articles

L. D. Landau. “The Bourgeoisie and Modern Physics”1), “Izvestia”, 23 November 1935. The text was written together with Korets and taken together to Bukharin, the editor of Izvestia. In the reprint there is one signature: Landau.

M. Korets. “The electron microscope”2). “Tekhnika Molodezhi”, 1937, no. 9, pp. 35–37.

E. Zelikovich and M. Korets. “With three twos”3), a puzzle story. “Tekhnika Molodezhi”, 1937, no. 9, pp. 61–62.

M. A. Karev. “Do Quarks Exist?”4). “Priroda”, 1966, no. 2, pp. 85–95.

S. V. Vladimirov, M. A. Karev. “Beyond the Two-Hundred-Degree Barrier. Directed Synthesis of Heat-Resistant Polymers”5). “Priroda”, 1966, no. 6, pp. 25–31.

S. V. Vladimirov, M. A. Karev. “Information and Us”6). Moscow, “Znanie”, 1970, 192 pp. Series “The People’s University”.

M. Korets, Z. Ponizovsky. “Why Is the Moon Not Made of Cast Iron?”7), “Kvant”, 1972, no. 4.
