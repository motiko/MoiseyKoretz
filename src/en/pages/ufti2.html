Order of arrest

Order

The city of Kharkov, 27.11.1935

I, Moroz, prosecutor for special cases of the Kharkov regional prosecutor’s office, having considered the submission of the Kharkov Regional Directorate of the NKVD for the arrest of Korets Moisey Abramovich, born 1908, an engineer, a former employee of UFTI, for whose arrest there is a sanction of the People’s Commissariat of Heavy Industry of the USSR under No. 18/9084 of 25.11 of this year

FOUND:

Korets M.A. is a participant in an underground counterrevolutionary group at UFTI, carries out work to undermine the institute and engages in counterrevolutionary organization, and therefore

RESOLVED:

The arrest of Korets Moisey Abramovich — to be sanctioned.

Moroz
