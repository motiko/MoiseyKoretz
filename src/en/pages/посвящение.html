Dedication

To N.P.

/ " Natasha" /

Musical performance

It was the summer of some year

/It seems, the twenty-eighth /.

Together with us nature laughed.

I remember,- we ourselves were nature.

It was summer, but it smelled of spring.

Toward midnight we went out of town

/I was nineteen/,

And the moon lit the mountains,

And the moon lit the mountains

And the silver minaret.

The poplars barely rustled their leaves

/We chattered, I don’t remember about what/,

And your eyes were shining,

Ah, your eyes were shining

With a fire that lights the soul.

It was a chance meeting

/So it seemed to me then /

I forgot the enchanting evening,

And did not remember that evening,

As in the morning one does not remember a dream.

But caught by the foul weather of life,

I understood, after a year of prison,

Here was where happiness was missed,

A whole life’s happiness was missed,

And we shall not meet it anymore.

In memory of our friendship that never came to be,

Loving late, in absence,

In the tender image of dear Natasha,

In the dear image of tender Natasha

I wanted to embody you.

1941
