Confrontation with Pyatigorsky

Record of the confrontation

Of the accused Korets Moisey Abramovich with the witness Pyatigorsky Leonid Moiseevich, of 28 December 1935.

Question to Korets: Do you know well the citizen sitting before you, and in what relations were you?

Korets’s answer: The citizen sitting before me I know well from the work at UFTI; his surname is Pyatigorsky; with him my relations were normal, as with all the staff of the institute.

Question to Pyatigorsky: What facts of anti-Soviet activity on the part of Korets Moisey Abramovich are known to you?

Pyatigorsky’s answer: I know that. Korets, while at the institute:

a) Engaged in disorganizing work at the institute, grouping other persons around himself on this basis.

b) Opposed the carrying out of defense orders by our institute, declaring that special defense work lowers the scientific level of the institute, and proposed that the same be divided into two parts.

c) Waging on this basis a struggle against the leadership of the institute, he declared that if we do not eat director Davidovich, then he will eat us, and the institute will come to ruin.

Question to Korets: Do you confirm the testimony of Pyatigorsky concerning you?

Korets’s answer: I confirm the testimony of the witness Pyatigorsky only in this, that I declared that if we do not eat Davidovich, then Davidovich will eat us. The rest I do not confirm.
