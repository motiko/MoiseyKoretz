The devil’s bonfire

Ernst P. – V.D.P.

Musical performance

When nature frowns,

Under the downpour the old pinewood shudders,

When foul weather catches

A campfire laid in the steppe,-

The red fire presses close to the earth,

Crawls in under the black coals,

And passionately begs protection

Of its mother – the earth.

But the wind, driving the clouds apart,

Will blow the dead litter from the logs,-

And again with a mighty flame

The fiery bonfire blazes.

…The devil himself, in a merry moment,

Scratching the back of his head with his spread hand,

Conceived a devilish prank

And brought you to me in hell.

And after that wild meeting

I love, as eight years ago,

Your languorous shoulders,

Your radiant eyes.

For this devilish torment,

Which cannot be put into verse,

Will you really not forgive the attempt

For an I-hour to forget my sins.

To forget the future and the past

And, if only you will be here,

To give me, simply as a keepsake,

A short sixty minutes.

And let the devils, shrieking, laugh

And drag me back into the inferno,-

Will save me from a vile death

A short sixty minutes.

3 June 1946
