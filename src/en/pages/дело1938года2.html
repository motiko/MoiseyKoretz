p.65 Record of a handwriting examination, 14.12.38

66 SECOND SPECIAL DEPARTMENT OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR A C T No. 334. Drawn up 14 December 38. On 14 December 1938, in the 2nd Department of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR, with a covering note No. 276401, material was received for the conduct of a handwriting ex- amination. As the suspected document there were submitted the orig- inal of a leaflet of c.r. content beginning with the words: "Comrad- es! The great cause of the October Revolution..." As an undoubted sample of writing for comparison there was submitted a manuscript of the suspect cit. KORETS in the form of an autobiog- raphy written by him in his own hand. The examination is required to establish whether the author of the c.r. leaflet is cit. KORETS M.A. EXAMINATION: The text of the c.r. leaflet is executed in green ink on a sheet of squared paper, torn from a school notebook. On examination of the handwriting of the c.r. leaflet and of the handwriting of the suspect- ed cit. KORETS there was established a whole series of common graphic fea- tures. 1. The handwriting of the c.r. leaflets and the handwriting of the suspect cit. KORETS — is right-slanting. The angle of slant is the same. 2. The direction of the line of writing in both texts is slightly wav- y, approaching a straight line. 3. The manner of joining letters to one another, and also of the main and hairline strokes — is arched. 4. The tension of the handwriting in both texts is 8-12 strokes. 5. The letters "д, з, у, р" in the one text and in the other are executed with a right-circular movement of the hand. 6. The capital letters "А, П, Р" are made with one movement of the hand. 7. The spread of the handwritings of both texts is, almost, the same. 8. The handwriting of the c.r. leaflet and the handwriting of the suspect are develop- ed. The degree of development is the same. 9. The letters: "а, д, ж, к, н, п, х, ц" are executed in both tex- ts with the same movement of the hand. CONCLUSION: The above-stated commonality of the graphic features of the c.r. leaflet and of the manuscript of the suspect KORETS allows us to draw the conclusion that the c.r. leaflet was executed by cit. KORETS M.A. [illegible] December 38. Expert: /KRASNOV/
