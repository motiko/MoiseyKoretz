This is the time ,when the author sat in prison, then in the camps, lived in barracks, with difficulty obtained paper, hiding these scraps wherever he could, went hungry, was tormented by the desire to smoke, having no tobacco, lay on the bunks, listening to the swearing around him, aching from loneliness and injustice – such is the background for his muse
