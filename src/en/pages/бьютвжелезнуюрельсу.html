They beat on a long iron rail

S.R.

Musical performance

They beat on a long iron rail

With their ringing they wake a longing in the soul…

I left my beloved

And went away from her across the river.

The snow held my clumsy felt boots,

The blizzard blinded my eyes,-

A little imp kept scraping at my heart

And whispered : " turn back".

But the ways back are closed to me,

I am left altogether alone.

And I drag along like a child punished

Unknown for what, and by whom.

How can I walk in submission,

How can I not curse fate,

If perhaps at this very moment

Someone is hurting you!?

I would gnaw through this iron rail,

I would smash any wall with my forehead,

And you, defenseless, tender,

I would shield with my body from them.

More unassailable than high walls,

And more terrible than a sea of fire,

That which now, my brown-eyed one,

Sets you apart from me.

A hammer beats on the long rail,

The ringing sounds with endless longing…

How am I to save my beloved,

Who is left alone beyond the river!

27 December 1945
