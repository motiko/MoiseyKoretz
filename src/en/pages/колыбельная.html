Lullaby

Musical performance

Sleep, my dear, at my breast,-

A whole hour lies ahead of us.

We may be just the two of us,-

Here we sing a little song.

A shadow falls from the houses,-

Oh, I am tired from the day:

All day long I must wait,

To see my little one.

The moon looks in at our window,-

It is all the same to you and me,

Now that we two are together,

We sing our little song.

The moon hangs on a bough, -

We shall ride beyond the river,

Beyond the great river we shall find

A pretty light-blue house.

The tiny one must fall asleep,

We shall travel a hard road,

And then, already the three of us,

We shall sing our little song.

15 May 1946
