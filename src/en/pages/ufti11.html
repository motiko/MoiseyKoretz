Interrogation record of Davidovich

Interrogation record

Witness Davidovich Semyon Abramovich, b. 1895, member of the CP(b)U, Tchaikovsky 16, apt. 12, 15.12.35.

Question: Tell what you know about the anti-Soviet subversive work of Korets, a former employee of UFTI.

Answer: Approximately in the month of March of this year our institute received a number of special assignments for defense work, the said assignments required, in their carrying out, a turn of our institute’s work toward technical themes alongside the scientific-theoretical work.

The former employee of the institute, Korets, after this became known to him, conducted subversive work against the carrying out of the defense assignments, which consisted in this, that on that ground he began to gather around himself individual specialists, especially foreign nationals, such as Weissberg and others, advancing, under the guise of improving the institute’s work, the idea of dividing it into three independent units, that the institute’s carrying out of the special assignments drags it down on the scientific side, that we ought to occupy ourselves more with theoretical work - under that guise Korets the whole time conducted demoralizing work among the scientific workers, concentrating all his attention on discrediting the leadership of the institute, that the latter is leading the institute to collapse, and that it is therefore necessary to wage a struggle against it.

Besides this. Korets came out in the institute’s wall newspaper with a proposal to lower the pay of those scientific workers who are directly engaged in carrying out defense work, for which he at that time had a corresponding penalty along Komsomol lines, and subsequently, for all his subversive work, Korets was expelled from the ranks of the VLKSM.

In his anti-Soviet work Korets closely formed a bloc with the foreign-national specialists, who supported him in every way.

Let us add that Korets together with his like-minded associates demanded the removal of Ryabinin, the only worker who was carrying out the defense work and in the event that their demands for the dismissal of Ryabinin were met, the most important defense work would have been disrupted.

Kozhevnikov
