I tore your image from my heart

V.D.P. – to Ernst P.

Musical performance

I tore your image from my heart,

With my foot I trampled the portrait.

You became so indifferently alien to me,

As if you were not there at all.

As if the Vitebsky station had never been,

Where, for me, under a salute of whistles,

A hundred times you whispered in my ear, in secret,

The burning word "I love you".

I live alone, serene and light,

And the days pass smoothly,

As on the night sea, far, far away,

The lights of the ship’s cabins.

And many interesting people around,

Handsome and clever men,

Beg for my smile as for a kindness,

And their lips are hot.

And I let myself be kissed,

And I smile at them,

Trying to forget that another woman, again,

Possesses your body.

By day, talk of her means nothing to me,-

Heartache is clamped in a vise,

But at night I dream of a heavy axe,

Crushing her temples.

Stay with her forever, if you will,

Coiled about you like a snake,-

I cannot, I do not want to be yours…

And yet I am yours.

28 May 1946
