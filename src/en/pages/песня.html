A song

Musical performance

You came to me by chance in the evening,

Looked at me with pain:

I can see it is very bleak for you

My dear, my beloved

Ah, how tired you were

In the light of the dying day, -

I know, there is no small blame in this,

There is no small blame of mine.

For I know how you were worn down

By the merciless points of fate

For I know how weary you have grown

Of howling forever like a wolf among wolves.

And already you imagine, beforehand,

That, wrecked by an evil lot,

The mangled building of a life

Has crushed the two of us to death.

My dear, my good one, my beloved!

Do not pine, do not torment your aching breast,-

Though the long road of life be hard,-

Together we will walk the heavy way.

And when, with glimmering dawns,

The southern fields are adorned,

We will fall asleep by azure shores,

My dear, my good one.

Liluli“R.S.V.”
