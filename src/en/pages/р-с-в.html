“R.S.V.”

Musical performance

The sea beats with a rumble against the cliffs,

Choking in the wild roar of the waves,

The waves bare a snarling maw

At a skiff someone left forgotten in the sea.

On its sides, with wondrous gilding,

By a craftsman's skillful hand,

Inlaid with love and with care

Is a precious mother-of-pearl design.

It might have been the finest ornament

Of a lake in a maharaja's gardens,

And from the very hour it was born

A quiet life was foretold for it.

But the northern winds began to blow,

An unexpected violent squall swept down,

And many thousands of kilometers off

Carried the light skiff away with it.

A faint echo of a southern song,

Forgotten music without words,

It flashes, a strip of gold,

Among the harsh, foaming billows.

And when by chance you come upon it

After long, gloomy, gray days,

As if you caught a ray of sun far off,-

It grows warmer in the heart.

27 July 1947
