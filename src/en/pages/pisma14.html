Letter fourteen

18.3.47. Mezhog -Leningrad

My dear little daughter!

In December 1946 I sent you a long letter a remittance for 50 rubles.But an answer I have still not received. I think that you answered, but your letter.probably did not reach me, and got lost, as these months I was gravely ill. I had a local infection of the blood ( a septic phlegmon of the floor of the mouth), and I had quite already made ready to die.But by chance it was possible to call a surgeon, and thanks to a rather complicated operation I remained alive. Only this Sunday I took off the bandage.As a reminder of this illness I am left with huge scars under the chin and a weakness that still does not leave me. In general one must consider that I luckily pulled through. Irusha, if my December letter has been kept by you, I beg you very much, answer it once more. All the questions,which I asked there., interest mE very much. As soon as I receive your answer to this letter, I will send again 50 rub.I understand that in the present situation this is a pittance, and I would like to help you more, but these rubles too are hard for me to gather. all the same I am very glad, yato I manage at least a little to help you and mama. To work in the laboratory I have already begun, but not a full day, as I tire quickly. But with every day my strength returns to me. Today I will limit myself to this short letter, but I hope that after your answer we will resume our correspondence with long letters. Then I will have more strength too. I kiss my dear little daughter tightly, your papa Misha.
