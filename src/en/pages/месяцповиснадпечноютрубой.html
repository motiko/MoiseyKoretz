The moon hung over the stovepipe

S.R.

Musical performance

The moon hung over the stovepipe,

Wiggling its little horns, its little legs.

Knows, the scamp, that I was with you

And that you kissed me.

He sees that I walk as if drunk,

Talking to myself,

He sees, - love rocks me as I go

With a burning languor.

Still he does not know how your caress

Leveled the boundaries between us,

How, hiding nothing, there welled in me

Life, a bright spring.

Darling! For today you were able

To kill the death in my heart.

Like a light mist, the gloom dispersed

Of our cruel fate.
