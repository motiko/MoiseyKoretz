Who is to blame?

I.I.P-u-V-u.

Musical performance

Are you to blame, or am I to blame?

Your heart, or my head?

Am I glad that you are going, or not glad?

Are you to blame, or am I to blame?

***

Surely you will not forget these meetings,

The tender warm Ukrainian evening,

The little sickle of the moon, the candles of the little stars,

Your hands, your lips, your shoulders…

You said, hiding your alarm:

“ My darling! Here she is, here I am!

My breast is under your hand.

Beloved, why then am I not all yours?”

Many, ah many, are the roads in the world.

Happiness I could not understand, could not grasp.

Did the devil muddle me, or God –

Only you I saved for afterwards.

Like a storm the eighteenth year was tearing

Into the city , the village, the mines , to the factory,-

The storm carried me higher, onward.

I saw Lenin…Close…So there…

The eyes of Ilyich , squinting a little, slyly:

“ Well then , lad, go work in the Cheka.

A true heart, the hand is firm,

Here, lad, for now you are needed by us.”

At once to the village I sent a little letter

Lida arrived, hides her face:

“ Darling! You are alive!...Only… You see – a ring.

Darling! I was deceived by a scoundrel.

He said that you were already no more.

Darling! I waited for you so many years!

I was only a day wrong…I made such trouble…

Beloved, will you forgive? Will you forgive , my light?

I said nothing, nothing,

Only… a mist filmed over the eyes.

Only…a tear rolled from the eyes.

Only… I took you to the station.

Are you to blame, or am I to blame?

The train has gone, -you cannot bring it back.

Only along the rails the wheels knock:

Who then is to blame, to blame, to blame?
