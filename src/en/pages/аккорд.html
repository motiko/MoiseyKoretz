A chord

G.E.

Musical performance

The sun today is glad

To bathe its rays in a puddle…

So you have no need,

Since you did not come again.

So a different joy

The day has brought for you…

Warm yourself alone in the sun,

Old, mangy dog!

10 April 1946

To a friendA strange evening
