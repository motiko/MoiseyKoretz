The prisoner’s questionnaire

The prisoner’s questionnaire

1. Korets

2. Moisey Abramovich

3. 12 November 1908

4. the city of Sevastopol, Yalta Street

5. Kharkov, Tchaikovsky 16, apt. 21

6. Scientific worker, physicist

7. UFTI, the university

8. Passport issued in Sverdlovsk, registered in Kharkov on 5.04.35

9. Father a craftsman, before the revolution he had a watch-repair workshop, he had one apprentice.

10. a) lived with relatives b) studied

11. Higher education, physicist

12. Belonged to the VLKSM from 1926 to November 1935, expelled for detachment from the Komsomol and for concealing his social origin.

13. A Jew, a citizen of the USSR.

1) Wife Epstein Eleonora Lazarevna, two small daughters, father — Abram Moiseevich, master at “Koopchas”, mother — Korets Slava Filippovna, resides in the city of Voronezh.

Arrested 28.11.35, 2nd Section, special block of the Kharkov Oblast Directorate of the NKVD.
