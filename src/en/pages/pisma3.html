Letter three

13/6/44

Mezhog.

My dear Shura and Irochka!

Yesterday I received your parcel and was moved by it to tears. For you are now the only people dear to me of whom I know anything at all, and the very fact that you answered my letter so quickly shows that I am dear to you. The feeling of loneliness and of being doomed is fading. If only I remain alive, I will repay your care a hundredfold in this hard moment for me. The most important thing in the parcel is the tobacco. Quite apart from my having suffered terribly myself with nothing to smoke, tobacco is greatly prized here. If it does not cost you too dearly, send more tobacco. Better less of something else.

Although now, after the harvest, things are probably easier for you. I have not yet received any letters from you. When I do, I will answer with a long letter, since you sent me paper and an envelope. The parcel took only ten days.

I kiss you both warmly, my dears.

Misha.
