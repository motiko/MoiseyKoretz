Song of the bears

Musical performance

( a dream )

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

The sun has set behind the mountain.

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

It is somehow cold in the pine wood.

By the river on the slope we are warm.

We watch how the people drive a long raft.

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

By the river on the slope we are warm.

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

Time goes slowly.

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

Today we ate honey.

And now we go to the Olekma to drink water.

By the Olekma the oaks rustle their leaves.

Oo-oo-oo. Oo-oo-oo.

We go to the river Olekma to drink water.

15 June 1946
