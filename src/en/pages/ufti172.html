Decision to arrest

NKVD of the Ukrainian SSR, Kharkov Regional Directorate,

3rd Department of the UGB, 5 July 1937, № 813321.

Top secret

3rd Section of the UNKVD for Voronezh Region

city of Voronezh.

According to the information we have, in the city of Voronezh there lives and works citizen Korets Moisey Abramovich, born in 1908, an engineer-physicist.

Korets was under development by us as a member of a counterrevolutionary Trotskyist wrecking organization.

In 1935 Korets was arrested by us; however, the guilt of the latter was not fully proved, in consequence of which Korets was not convicted and the case against him was closed.

At present we have begun the liquidation of the entire counterrevolutionary wrecking group at UFTI, and from the investigation materials obtained by us it has been established that Korets is one of the active participants in the said counterrevolutionary group and the closest friend of the leader of this group, the Trotskyist Professor Landau.

Korets has been designated by us for arrest.

We request that you urgently locate Korets M.A., take him before the arrest into active agent servicing, and inform us of all materials obtained there.

Deputy chief of the UNKVD for Kharkov Region Grishin

Deputy chief of the 3rd Section Tornuev
