Fergana

Musical performance

Catching up with the white-faced moon,

The sun brightly lit Fergana,

Rose above the snowy comb of the mountains

Into the dark-blue dazzling expanse,

Catching up with the white-faced moon.

Like a maiden-bride, Fergana

Stretched, waking from sleep,

Smiling at the dream not dreamed out,

Girls look out the window at Fergana:-

Like a maiden-bride Fergana.

Smiling at the dream not dreamed out,

Young men look out the window at Fergana.

Like a maiden-bride at the window,

Fergana beckons them with a sweet tryst,

Smiling at the dream not dreamed out.

The sun scattered generous gifts,-

An aryk sparkled with a diamond stream,

On the scarlet clusters of grapes along the mountain,

On the swarthy faces of the white-toothed children

The sun scattered generous gifts.

Filled the gardens with a ringing song

Crowds of girls gathering the fruit.

The branches are breaking from the ripening fruit

Hundreds of joyful, merry voices

Filled the gardens with a ringing song.

Someone’s swarthy shoulder flashed by,

A young man’s heart began to beat hot,

Because among the whitewashed trunks,

As if come out of the night’s teasing dreams,

Someone’s swarthy shoulder flashed by.

The sun scattered itself in patches in the bushes,

A kiss trembles on tender lips.

The tender dream not dreamed out will be dreamed through,

That in the morning was remembered at the windows…

A ray of the sun will not disturb them in the bushes…

There is no land on earth more radiant,

Than the one that spreads in the valley of Fergana.

There a frozen soul will grow warm,

There a dream breathes easily and sweetly.

There is no land on earth more radiant.

Dear one, if you are in Fergana,

At golden noon remember me.

25 December 1946
