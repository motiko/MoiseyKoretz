The priest

Musical performance

The marble grows rosy from the sun.

And down a staircase of a hundred steps

The priest descends from the temple

To admire the play of shadows.

A white lotus opens,

The yellow Nile stirs the reeds,

And he tries to hear something

The High Priest of the evening hush.

That Ra might send down victory

To the war chariots of the father,

He as a three-year-old child was given

Into the hands of Amon’s wise priests.

He came to know the endlessness of mystery,

Trembling beside the sacred fire,

The inspiration of chance discoveries

And a high passion for science.

All that can be embraced and measured

And marked with an impassive number,

All that can be understood and verified,-

He verified with a fearless mind.

Indifferent to suffering and to pain,

Contemplating good and evil,

With the shackles of an unsleeping will

He bound his restless flesh.

Like the divine falcon of Thoth

He soared into the blue height,

Affirming high above the world

An all-conquering- free thought.

But when the shadows gather

For the evening play of the sun,

The priest goes down upon his knees

And anguish creeps into the breast.

Again he wants to overhear something,

That has remained the secret of the gods,-

But by the angry god of the flesh,

Like an apostate, for ages of ages.

He is condemned in the desert of consciousness,

With filigree thoughts- beads,

To perish in the solitude of knowledge

Boundless, shapely, precise.

5 July 1946
