The heady scent of spring grasses

Musical performance

The heady scent of spring grasses

Will languish as an evening song…

Night passes on velvet paws

Over the earth, tired of pain.

Night, a soundless tender cat,

Has gone around the corner barrack,

Has looked in at my window…

Has lowered the heavy curtain,

Has lain down on the table by the candle,-

Every evening at the appointed hour

We remain face to face.

There is no hiding from her anywhere:-

A keen narrow green pupil

Searches hotly on the white page

For new lines written in blood.

Out of the corners the shadows crawl,

Look piteously and sharply,

Wait, having embraced my knees,

For new lines written in blood.

A little old gnome came out from behind the stove,

Quietly sat on the threshold behind my back,

To hear the indistinct speech

Of new lines written in blood.

Silence.

And a song is born,

A Palace of words is raised,

And in the soul there blooms a wondrous,

Ineffably beautiful flower.

It lifts its pure corolla,

Like lunar silver.

And from its fragrant petals

Blood drips onto the paper.

3 May 1947
