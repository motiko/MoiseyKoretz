Night

/ "Natasha"/

Musical performance

Is this the little room, or is it not?

Is it you, Natashenka , or is it not?

Where is your youth, where is your dream?

Is it you, Natashenka, or is it not?

She did not bring her body as a gift

And was raped by her own beloved.

God, my God, how is she to live from now on?

Who will help her, who will understand her?

Even Seryozha, even Sergei,

Will turn away too and will not be with her.

Mama, dear, how can I tell you?

Anxious eyes will see it all at once!

Mama, dear, darling , forgive me!

Only do not sorrow, only do not grieve!

No. Natasha cannot earn forgiveness.

Better for her to die, better not to live!

She wipes the tears with a lock of hair,

Has bitten the tips of her braids with her lips,

Pain cuts the heart sharper than a knife,

Natashenka weeps – her little shoulders shake.

Natashenka weeps, a tear on her cheek,

Tired eyes stick heavily shut,

Her head lies on the wet pillow,

Poor Natashenka falls asleep…sleeps…

Sleep my Natashenka, my little dove.

By the little white bed I will stand.

Sleep, my Natashenka, lie quietly,

Tomorrow there will be morning, –tomorrow there will be life!

1940
