Bach

“ Amid the horrors of war, which more than once laid waste his small but fervently loved homeland, in the fire of the conflagrations that destroyed his last possessions, amid the deaths of near and dear people, burdened with a numerous family, weighed down by the grave illness of his beloved companion, bending under the blows of want and hunger, the forgotten organist of a provincial Catholic church, the great Johann Sebastian Bach, created his mighty immortal works, full of joy and of the praise of life, which for hundreds of years awaken in the souls of people vigor, a striving toward the great, and a boundless love for all-conquering life”.

2 November 1946
