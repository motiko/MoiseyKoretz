Landau’s letter to the People’s Commissar of Internal Affairs of Ukraine

31.12.35

Dear Comrade Balitsky1!

I appeal to you with a request to intervene in the examination of the case of an employee of the Ukrainian Physico-Technical Institute, the engineer Korets, arrested on 28 November of this year. Comrade Korets was, over the past year, my closest colleague. I knew him well in private life as a man infinitely devoted to Soviet power. Together with him we set ourselves the task of doing everything in our power so as to make science in our country the first in the world. I absolutely cannot imagine that this man could have done anything hostile to the policy of the Party. I did not succeed in learning anything definite about the reasons for his arrest. I cannot help connecting it with the activity of the former director Davidovich. Inside the institute Davidovich created an atmosphere of dirty intrigues and crude persecution. The majority of the institute's principal employees consider that Davidovich is wrecking the institute, and they lodged with the central bodies a request for his removal. In response to this, Davidovich tried in every place and everywhere to present the matter as if the institute's employees were fighting not against him, but against the special work assigned to the institute. In particular, Davidovich spread such accusations with regard to me, and therefore I can affirm with full responsibility that they are a crude lie, having no basis whatsoever in actual reality. At present these outrageous accusations have fallen away with the removal of Davidovich and the appointment, by the Central Committee of the VKP(b), of Leipunsky in his place. I have no doubt that Davidovich and his assistants could systematically mislead the organs of the NKVD and, not finding it convenient to slander me too crudely, tried to present my closest colleague and assistant as the head of a conspiracy. All of Comrade Korets's activity at UFTI passed before my eyes, and I am ready at any time to give exhaustive testimony concerning him. I would very much ask you, if you find it possible, to give me an occasion to talk about Korets's case in a personal conversation with you.

Scientific director of the theoretical department of UFTI

L.D. Landau
