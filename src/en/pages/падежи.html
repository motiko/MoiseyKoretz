The cases

Musical performance

Good morning, dear Chizh!

They write to me that you are naughty,

And therefore Chizha

They have stopped respecting.

If it is true, then to Chizhu

I will tell no fairy tales.

If you start to argue,

I will stop loving Chizha altogether.

I am displeased with Chizhom

And I do not think of him.

I have already forgotten

About my own dear Chizhe.

I want all the Chizhi

To know the cases firmly.

12 May 1946

Inscription on a child’s drawingLullaby
