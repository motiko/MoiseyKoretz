Song of a street child

Musical performance

Train, hoot, tap, knock,

Race from slope to slope.

Someone’s legs, arms can be seen

Between the hubs of the wheels…

Sleepers –cursed rails,

Black days and nights,

Our lives- busted .

The years without a home.

Ah-ah, little lady with the flowers:

Allow me to help you up,

Give a little kopeck for a keepsake,

Or else I’ll spit in your mug once!

And into the rich pockets-

Nimble little fingers,

Our lives busted.

The years without a home.

So yesterday Khvedka fell off,

The wheel chewed him up.

His head split like a radish,

And his whole face without skin…

So I’ll fall off too, boys,

Down onto the black rails,

And so mine will be crushed

The years without a home!...

1939
