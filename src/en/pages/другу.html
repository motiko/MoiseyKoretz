To a friend

To F.Prokopov

Musical performance

It was not one mother who bore you and me,

It was not one sister who loved us,-

So what devilish force was it

That made kin of you and me forever,

My dear, my comrade and brother.

Was it that you and I together

Were here, at the edge of life,

Was it that at death, as at a bride,

You and I looked into the eyes together

As at a bride awaited our death.

Was it that vicious snowstorms

Blinded the stupor of sleepy eyes,

Was it that on one bed

Of the branches of a prickly stiff spruce

You and I fell asleep more than once.

We know how hunger silently strangles

Will, memory, honesty and love,

We know how thirst dries the throat, -

With a cough we hawked up the soul

And with the soul spat out blood.

But whatever clouds may have hung,

However they might slash our fate,-

All the same,- we could not but think,

With a furious fever of thought

An unquenchable spirit blazed in us.

For hundreds of years this flame does not go out,

Lighting the way of the cross of the ages.

Many of the dead and the unborn with us

This flame soldered into one alloy,

Many thoughts, souls, ideas, names…

tomorrow you will leave closer to the sun

And, perhaps, for you fate,

Or that which moves our life,

Will string a chain of joyful days

And will wipe the brand of a slave from the face.

Only the years will not overcome friendship,

Whether you are glad of it or not glad,-

Because the force is indestructible

That welded you with me into one,

My dear , my comrade and brother.

6 March 1946
