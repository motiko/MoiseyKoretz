New Year

Musical performance

The gray-haired old man changes his name again,

And stirs the mist of what is to come,

And again the tried deceit of the numerals

And the foolish swarm of hopes born of them.

In what is to come we seek the face of happiness unrealized,

But the distance remains dirty-gray,

But the old sadness remains

And the familiar foul weather of our sullen days.

Impassive clocks tick out the years.

Inexorable is the iron running of the times.

And, ending our life’s dream,

The centuries will close the pitiless vaults.

Where, then, to find the lost years?

How to patch a life torn apart?...

Kill the cry with a pillow, lie like a mute corpse,

Without thought, without hopes, without happiness, without freedom.
