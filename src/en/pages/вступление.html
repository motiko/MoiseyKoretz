Introduction

Time in a furious torrent

Flows across the universe

And carries off, never to return,

Much joy and much grief.

Like a black snag

In the muddy waters of the Zeravshan,

Now flying upward, like a ball,

Now settling on the thick bottom

Beats furiously against the rocks, -

So I too, in the stream of life,

For nearly half a century now,

Beat against human stupidity

And against the hard stones

Of fanatical ideas.

At about fifteen I began

To study the volumes of the works

Of the wisest word-mongers

From Socrates to Spinoza.

I read Kant with painful effort,

And Hegel, too, I got through,

Even Kolman and Maksimov

Were not forgotten by me.

To complete the collection,

I read the Quran and the Torah,

And the Rig- Veda in an abridged

Very free translation,

And Thomas Aquinas.

After many years of work,

I reached the cherished goal,

To which there did not break through

A single ancient alchemist.

All the alchemists sought

The wondrous philosopher's stone,

Which, by a single touch,

Turned any shit

Into precious metals.

Under a mountain of terminologies,

From under the rubbish of debates,

Shone with a radiant gleam

The pure philosophical stone.

The look of an enormous crystal,

With a flawlessly exact facet,

It had. My find,

I confess, disconcerted me.

A conscientious analysis

Explained the nature of the stone: -

Human stupidity

Of a crystalline kind.

The component elements: -

A priori ideas,

All sorts of preconceived opinions

And the obstinacy of fanaticism.

As the alchemists had expected,

This stone, if one so wished,

The appearance of cast gold

Imparted to any objects.

But, however, in addition,

Contrary to every expectation,

The wondrous crystal possessed

And a reverse, terrible power.

To precious revelations,

To the inspired pearls of thought

Touching, it instantly

Turned them into filthy rubbish...

Tpr-r-ru, Pegasus! Where have you ridden?

The devil has carried you off to the ravines!

I had no intention at all

Of discoursing on this stone.

And what is it to anyone

About philosophical crystals!

So here I set a full stop, -

Ride in, Pegasus, from the start.

To the readerPrologue
