Extract from the Kharkov regional court record

Extract from record no. 9

of the administrative session of the special collegium

of the Kharkov regional court 18.02.36

Composed of chairman Dmitruk and the members of the collegium Fadeev and Shevchenko, with secretary Tatar, with the participation of prosecutor Moroz. Rapporteur Comrade Moroz

Heard: case no. 147 on the charge against Korets Musiy Abramovich under Art.97 of the Criminal Code of the UkrSSR. The case was received on 25.01.36.

Resolved: To consider that the preliminary investigation has been conducted with sufficient completeness. The crime has been qualified correctly. Since there are in the case materials which cannot be discussed in open session, the case is to be set for hearing in closed session on 26.02.36 at 10 in the morning in the city of Kharkov. Taking into account that the materials of the case are of an exclusively secret character, and guided by the directive letter of the People's Court and the Procuracy of the Union under no. 23 of 13.06.35, the case is to be heard without the participation of the parties. The lists of witnesses are to be confirmed. The preventive measure is to be left as before — detention in custody.

Tatar
