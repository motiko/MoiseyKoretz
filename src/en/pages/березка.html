The little birch

/ " Natasha"/

Musical performance

I got up early today,

Ran off through the dew:

Didn’t even braid the ribbons

Into the plait coming loose.

The path winds like a fishing line,

Runs on as far as the little wood.

Hello, dear little birch!

I have come to look for you.

See, the day has raised its curtain,

The tender dawn is over.

You know, for me and for you today

Sixteen years apiece will come.

Now I am your namesake,-

Don’t look down like that from on high:

He said that I am a little birch,

And beautiful, like you…

Ah, why do you drop

A wet leaf on my neck!...

Yes, little birch! You don’t know,-

He is a terrible pessimist.

He reasons so cleverly,

As you and I cannot:

Says that he denies

The joy of being in the world,

That all culture is worthless,

That all art is hackwork,

That literature is empty,

Drama, painting, sculpture,

That people’s nature is vile…

…And he wanted to embrace me!

Well, and I am such a fool:

I laughed – and ran!..

And then I sat the whole night,-

Sleep was out of the question,-

Softly I sang songs

At the open window…

He will come again today,

I will bring him here,

And so that I may be quick-witted,

You prompt me then.

And I will say, wisely, wisely:

Hear the joy of being:

This- is the sun, this-morning,

This –youth, this –I!
